About

Allen Usubov is an Art Director & Designer born and based out of New York City. With experience across branding, apparel, product design, and e-commerce, he has led creative from early concept through final production. With over 7 years of design experience, he has also directed design for brands such as Sinclair Global & Golfwang. His approach prioritizes consistency, taste, and detail to ensure every element supports a cohesive vision.
